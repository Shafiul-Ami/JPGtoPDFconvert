FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device, and the tool keeps working even without an internet connection once the page is loaded.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG / JPEG, PNG and WEBP. Transparent areas in PNG images are filled with white.

How many images can I convert at once?

There's no fixed limit. Very large batches depend on your device's memory — a few hundred photos is usually fine on a modern computer.

What does "Image quality" do?

Lower quality makes the PDF smaller, which is handy for emailing. "High" keeps photos sharp with a reasonable file size.
